Featured Event

Sponsorship Night 2026

14 October 2026 | 6:00 pm–8:00 pm   La Maison, Albury

Join Business Women Albury Wodonga for a special evening celebrating the organisations and individuals who helped make 2026 possible and welcoming those interested in partnering with us in 2027.

Sponsorship Night is an opportunity to connect with our business community, recognise the valued support of our current sponsors and learn more about the exciting sponsorship opportunities available for the year ahead.

Whether you supported us throughout 2026 or would like to discover how your business can become involved in 2027, we would love you to join us for a relaxed evening of conversation, connection and celebration.

Come along, raise a glass and discover how we can work together to support and strengthen our regional business community.

BWAW events help women build lasting relationships, find new opportunities and feel part of a supportive community.

Meet peers, share ideas and learn from others. Our events make it easier to connect, find mentors and encourage one another.

Join a community where women support one another and turn new connections into opportunity and motivation.

  1. Everyone is welcome at our events and workshops.If you’d rather not come alone, contact us and we’ll arrange a buddy.
  2. Members of Albury Business Connect and Business Wodonga receive discounted rates.Membership isn’t required to attend.
  3. Event fees are non-refundable within five business days of the event.If BWAW cancels, fees are refunded. If an event is rescheduled, you can request a refund or transfer your booking.
