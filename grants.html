BWAW Grant

Since 2023, the BWAW Grant has supported women in business across Albury-Wodonga.

We’re pleased to continue the program after its success.

  • The grant provides financial support to local women business owners who want to start, grow or develop their business.
  • Recipients can access resources, funding and local connections to support their next steps.

We look forward to strengthening our local business community and helping women thrive.

2025 Grant Finalists

Style Revive

Style Revive has been servicing the women of our community by educating them to feel empowered and confident to not only get their inner colour back but express it through their personal style, they help women get their inner colour (sense of self) back through their personal style.They deliver their services both in person and virtually.

Johara Co.

Johara Co. delivers high quality, sustainable leisure wear and self care products that promote well being and relaxation. They design and oversee the manufacturing of all clothing items and select the finest of products to stock. Their offerings include luxurious robes, essential oil blends, bath soaks, and more.
