Business Women Albury Wodonga (BWAW)

brings women in business across our region together, so no one has to do it alone.

We offer a welcoming place for honest conversations, connection and lasting relationships.

Founded in 2009 to support women in business across Albury-Wodonga.

BWAW is an initiative of Albury Business Connect and Business Wodonga. Together, we strengthen and support our local business community.

Our network has grown into a trusted place for connection, professional growth and lasting relationships.

A community built on connection, not competition

Our community comes from many industries, roles and stages of business.

  • Some are just starting out.
  • Some are growing established businesses.
  • Some are leading teams, juggling family, or reinventing their careers.

We’re united by how we support one another.

Events and shared conversations help members feel seen, supported and inspired to grow.

Come as you are

You don’t need it all worked out or to feel “networky”. Just come as you are.

Here, women share stories, build relationships and support one another in business and beyond.

We’d love to welcome you into the BWAW community.

Our purpose

Our vision is to contribute to a thriving local business community by empowering women through:

  • CONNECTION AND COMMUNITY
  • MEANINGFUL EVENTS AND CONVERSATIONS
  • COMMUNICATION AND SHARED LEARNING
  • SUPPORT THAT REFLECTS THE REAL NEEDS OF WOMEN IN BUSINESS

We’re passionate about creating an environment where women feel confident, encouraged, and supported to grow, not just in business, but as leaders, decision makers, and contributors to our region.
