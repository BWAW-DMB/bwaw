How to become a
member of BWAW?

As a member of either Albury Business Connect or Business Wodonga, or as an employee of a business that is a member, you automatically become part of the BWAW community.

Join BWAW
This membership grants you access to:
  • exclusive events
  • networking opportunities
  • chance to connect with like-minded women in business.

Both Albury Business Connect and Business Wodonga offer fantastic value with their memberships – if you would like to join either Chamber find out more here:

Business Wodonga – Membership Benefits

Business Wodonga work with members and the local business community, we offer a variety of opportunities for businesses in our region to connect and engage with one another, to be your voice and to provide learning and development opportunities.

Whether through one of our events, workshops or referrals, Business Wodonga and our greater network is here to support you in business.

View Website

Albury Business Connect – Membership Benefits

Whether it’s a workshop on how to improve your cash flow, or a link to resources on winning government tenders, Albury Business Connect members have access to plenty of revenue boosting resources and activities.

Our Business Before Hours and Business After Hours events are some of the most well-known ways we support local businesses, you can make new connections and forge new partnerships to find your next opportunity.

18-40 years old – Looking To Connect with Like Minded People?

Young Business Edge – Learn More

Young Business Edge is a sub-committee of Albury Business Connect and Business Wodonga, creating networking and professional development opportunities for young professionals and business owners between ages of 18 and 40 in our region.
